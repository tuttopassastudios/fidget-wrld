# Compliance Checklist – U.S. Magnet-Ball Fidget Product (2026)

Use this checklist while building your Shopify/online store pages and before launching in the U.S. market.

---

## 1. Product and Legal Classification

- [ ] Confirm the product is **not marketed or advertised as a toy for children**.
- [ ] If selling in the U.S., ensure the product either:
  - [ ] **Meets 16 CFR 1262 (magnet-safety standard)**, **or**
  - [ ] Is clearly labeled as **adult-only, not a toy**.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation or test reports from your supplier that:
  - [ ] The magnets meet **16 CFR 1262** and/or **ASTM F963-23** (if applicable).
  - [ ] The **exact SKU is not currently recalled** by CPSC or other agencies.

---

## 2. Age Restrictions and Warnings (On-Page)

- [ ] Add a visible **age statement** on the product page such as:
  - [ ] "Not a toy. Intended for adults 14+ / 18+ only."
- [ ] Include a **bold ingestion-hazard warning** such as:
  - [ ] "Keep away from children. If swallowed, multiple magnets can cause serious injury or death. Seek medical attention immediately."
- [ ] Repeat key warnings on:
  - [ ] Product description.
  - [ ] Cart / checkout page or notice near "Add to cart."
  - [ ] FAQ or Policies / Safety page.

---

## 3. Store-Level Disclosures and Policies

- [ ] Create a **Product Safety / Hazards** page with:
  - [ ] A clear section on magnet-ball risks (choking, ingestion, internal injury).
  - [ ] A note that the product is not intended for children or pets.
- [ ] Add a **Terms and Conditions** clause that:
  - [ ] States the product is **for adults only**.
  - [ ] Disclaims liability for misuse or for use by minors.
- [ ] Add a **Shipping / Returns / Recall** note that:
  - [ ] States you will cooperate with any future recalls or CPSC notices.

---

## 4. Supplier and Inventory Controls

- [ ] Confirm that your drop-ship / fulfillment partner:
  - [ ] Does **not supply recalled magnet-ball SKUs** (e.g., GLMZZ, Huaker, etc.).
  - [ ] Will **notify you immediately** if a product is recalled or pulled.
- [ ] Regularly check:
  - [ ] CPSC recalls page for "magnetic ball," "magnet set," "fidget magnet ball."
  - [ ] Any recall notices for your specific product or similar SKUs.

---

## 5. Ads, Marketing, and Naming

- [ ] Ensure ad copy and social media:
  - [ ] **Does not call the product a "toy" for kids.**
  - [ ] Avoids imagery that shows children playing with or mouth-handling the magnets.
- [ ] Avoid using:
  - [ ] "kids," "children," "toddlers," "school," "classroom" in main product titles or hero copy.
- [ ] If using UGC or influencer content, provide them:
  - [ ] A brief **safety disclaimer template** for captions/stories.

---

## 6. Checkout and Customer Experience

- [ ] Add a **modal or banner** at checkout (if possible) for this product:
  - [ ] "This product contains powerful magnets and is intended for adults only. Keep away from children."
- [ ] Consider:
  - [ ] An **age-gate pop-up** or checkbox for age confirmation.
- [ ] Log any **customer support tickets** related to safety or misuse for future audit/defense.

---

## 7. Ongoing Compliance Fire-Drill

- [ ] Set up a **monthly check**:
  - [ ] Run a quick search for "CPSC recall magnetic ball" and your product name/brand.
  - [ ] If anything new appears, pause sales and consult legal or compliance support.
- [ ] Keep a small **compliance folder** with:
  - [ ] Supplier test reports or compliance letters.
  - [ ] Screenshots of your on-page warnings and age statements.
